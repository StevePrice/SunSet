# Sunset Soul logo

The Sunset Soul logo is **Last Light · Beams**: a sun setting behind the curve of the earth, with seven tapered beams of light. The wordmark is Fraunces, with "Sunset" in SemiBold and "Soul" in Medium Italic, apricot.

The wordmark has been converted to shapes, so the files look the same everywhere with no font installed.

## Which file to use

All files are in this folder. Each comes as **SVG** (sharp at any size; for code, web and print) and **PNG** (a regular image; for uploads, slides and social media).

| Where | File |
|---|---|
| App screens, website header, emails, documents on a light background | `sunset-soul-logo-horizontal-transparent-for-light-backgrounds` |
| The same, on a navy or dark background | `sunset-soul-logo-horizontal-transparent-for-dark-backgrounds` |
| Square or tall spaces (splash screen, posters, profile banners) | `sunset-soul-logo-stacked-transparent-for-light-backgrounds` / `…-for-dark-backgrounds` |
| Slides or social posts where the background should come with the logo | `…-on-navy` / `…-on-cream` versions of either layout |
| The symbol alone, where the name already appears nearby | `sunset-soul-mark-for-light-backgrounds` / `…-for-dark-backgrounds` |

### App icons and small sizes (`app-icons/`)

| Where | File |

The app icons are full squares on purpose: Apple and Google round the corners themselves. Don't round them first.

## Rules

- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo at least the height of the sun.
- **Minimum size:** the horizontal logo at least 120px wide; below 64px, use the small icon.
- **Match the background:** "for light backgrounds" files on white, cream or light photos; "for dark backgrounds" files on navy or dark photos.
- **Don't** stretch, squash, tilt, recolor, outline, or add shadows, glows or other effects.
- **Don't** rebuild the wordmark in a font or rearrange the mark and the name.

## The logo on photos

- **Never** put the logo on a person's photo inside the app (Journal, Entry Detail, Live Map). Those photos are the user's memories.
- **On images shared outside the app** (the share card, Instagram and Facebook Stories cards), put the mark in the photo's **top-left corner**: `sunset-soul-mark-for-dark-backgrounds.svg` at 34pt wide, centered on a 48pt square of translucent navy, `rgba(22, 34, 74, 0.55)`, with 10pt rounded corners, 12pt in from the edges (24pt on full-screen Stories cards).
- On those shared images the **score badge goes in the bottom-right corner**, so the mark and the score balance each other diagonally.

## Before printing or filing

Sunrise and sunset marks are common. Do a trademark search before printing or registering the logo.
