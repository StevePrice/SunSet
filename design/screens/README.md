# Sunset Soul screen pictures (Phase 4e)

Still pictures of every screen in the Phase 4e designs, in dark mode and light mode, so you (or Claude Code) can look at a screen right next to the [Build Spec](../../sunset-soul-build-spec-flow-fixes.md) while building it.

- **The live designs** are the source of truth: https://claude.ai/artifact/XCsz8MDaV7vdQPb6UM5Wd2 (private to Louise until she shares it). These pictures are a snapshot from November 9, 2026. If a picture and the Build Spec disagree, follow the Build Spec and ask Louise.
- Pictures are 2× phone size (780 px wide, drawn at 390 px). Tall screens are shown in full, as if scrolled.
- The photos are the design system's sample photos; titles, the Wow lines and the friend "Maria" are examples.
- The © line at the bottom of every screen isn't drawn (see Build Spec 2.2).
