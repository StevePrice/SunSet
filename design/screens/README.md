# Sunset Soul screen pictures (Phase 4e and 4f)

Still pictures of every screen in the Phase 4e designs, in dark mode and light mode, so you (or Claude Code) can look at a screen right next to the [Build Spec](../../sunset-soul-build-spec-flow-fixes.md) while building it. Screens 17–26 are new in Phase 4f ([Sunsets Together Build Spec](../../sunset-soul-build-spec-sunsets-together.md)); screens 03, 04, 05, 08, 10, 11, 12 and 13 were updated for it.

- **The live designs** are the source of truth: https://claude.ai/artifact/XCsz8MDaV7vdQPb6UM5Wd2 (private to Louise until she shares it). These pictures are a snapshot from November 9, 2026, with the Phase 4f screens and updates added November 22, 2026. If a picture and a Build Spec disagree, follow the Build Spec (Phase 4f over 4e) and ask Louise.
- Pictures are 2× phone size (780 px wide, drawn at 390 px). Tall screens are shown in full, as if scrolled.
- The photos are the design system's sample photos; titles, the Wow lines the friends "Steve" and "Maria", and their memories are examples. The sample photos don't show people; real ones would.
- The © line at the bottom of every screen isn't drawn (see Build Spec 2.2).
