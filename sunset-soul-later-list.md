## When it's a native app (App Store and Google Play, Phase 9)
Sunset Soul is a web app for the MVP. These features need, or work much better in, a native phone app.
- **Photos save to the phone automatically:** every photo taken in the app is copied to the phone's photo library, built into the phone with no extra step (the phone asks permission once). In the MVP, photos taken in the app stay in the Journal; a "Save to your photos" step after every photo was tried and dropped on November 22 because it slowed people down.
- **"From today" photo strip:** at the top of the New sunset choose-a-photo step, a row of photos taken in the last few hours, so someone who just took ten shots of tonight's sunset can tap the best one without searching.
- **We found your sunsets, full version:** the app finds the sunsets in the camera roll for you. (The simple pick-many version can come sooner; see Sunsets Together.)
- **One-tap Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok and Facebook buttons** on the share panel.
- **Sunset reminders** that work reliably (PRD 6.8; on a web app they need the app installed to the home screen on iPhone).
- **Notification when a friend reacts.**
- **Home-screen widget** for a close circle (Locket-style).

## Sunsets Together — Phase 2
The MVP part (names, the With people filter, Sunsets with [Name], Two sides of the story typed or spoken, Share it in your own voice, "You're in Louise's sunset" and "I was there!") is in PRD 6.10 and the Phase 4f Build Spec.
- **Gift a sunset:** send a sunset as a card on a chosen date (anniversary, birthday, a hard day), with a personal message.
- **Afterglow keepsake:** save the photo, story, reactions and friends' memories as one keepsake page. (Afterglow is the internal name; in the app the screen is "From your friends".)
- **A Share Page that opens like a sunset:** the photo warms from dusk to full color when a friend opens the link.
- **We found your sunsets, simple version:** pick many sunset photos at once on day one; they land with real dates and places, AI titles and Wow lines. (The full version is under "When it's a native app".)

## Finding Your Sunsets — Phase 2
Uses photo and story "fingerprints" saved at each entry (optional in the MVP, PRD 6.10a), plus dates, places and names.
- **Ask your Journal:** search in everyday words ("the beach with the grandkids", "after Dad's birthday").
- **More like this:** a row of similar sunsets on Entry Detail.
- **Pick the best one:** suggest the best of several nearly identical shots.
- **Automatic collections:** by sky (On fire, Pastels, Stormy, Last light), by place (Ocean, Mountains, Desert, City), by people moment (Silhouettes, Family, Just the two of us, Kids, Pets) and by trip.
- **Same spot over the years:** every sunset from one favorite place, side by side.
- **Look-alike sunsets on the Live Map** (public posts only, once the map exists).
- Never: face recognition or face fingerprints.

## Your collection (for people with lots of sunsets)
Best bundled together once real users have large Journals.
- **Favorites:** mark a sunset as a favorite and see only favorites in the Journal. Use a **star**, not a heart (the heart already means "Love this!").
- **Jump to:** a button in the Photos view that lists years and months, so nobody has to scroll through hundreds of photos.
- **Share several sunsets at once:** pick a few in the Photos view (say, five from one trip) and send them together. (Sharing every sunset with one person is in the MVP as Sunsets with [Name]; this is the free-choice version.)
- **On this day:** "A year ago today…" memories of past sunsets.
- **Season recaps:** a look back at a season's sunsets (Spotify Wrapped-style), including "Your sunset people this summer".

## Capturing and storytelling
- **Several photos per sunset** (a carousel).
- **Golden-hour daily moment:** a nudge to capture tonight's sunset (BeReal-style).
- **Rare Sightings badges:** "Sun rays", "Alpenglow", "Green flash" and others, with a collection in the Journal. The best candidate for something on the photo, instead of a number.
- **"Be in it" capture tip:** a gentle suggestion to turn around and take a silhouette photo against the light.
- (Sunset reminders and the "From today" strip moved to "When it's a native app".)

## Friends and sharing
- **Friends / all reactions in one place:** a bottom-bar tab showing every reaction and note across all your shared sunsets, newest first.
- **Send one back:** a friend replies with their own sunset.
- **Reply to a friend's reaction.**
- **Dead-link page:** a kind message when a share link was stopped or the sunset deleted.
- **In-app friend circles** and a **public story feed** (Phase 2).
- (Voice from friends moved into the MVP as part of Two sides of the story, PRD 6.10e. One-tap social buttons, friend-reaction notifications and the home-screen widget moved to "When it's a native app".)

## Live Map
- **Live Map and "Post to map"** (Phase 6, now after Phase 4f). The bottom bar grows from Journal · You to Journal · Map · You (and a Friends tab when that arrives). New sunset stays at the top of the screen.
- **People check before posting:** photos with people stay off the public map by default, and photos of children are never allowed (PRD 6.10).
- **Community voting** that keeps great sunsets on the map longer.
