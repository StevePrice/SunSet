# Sunset Soul — Later List
**Started:** November 8, 2026, by Louise.
**What this is:** features we've decided are *not* in the MVP, kept in one place so good ideas aren't lost. Each one is designed right before it's built, using the design system and the flow map.
**Related:** [Build Spec, Phase 4e](sunset-soul-build-spec-flow-fixes.md) · [PRD](sunset-soul-prd.md) "Out of Scope" (section 8) · [Concept doc](sunset-soul-concept.md) "Later (v2+)".

---

## Your collection (for people with lots of sunsets)
Best bundled together once real users have large Journals.
- **Favorites:** mark a sunset as a favorite and see only favorites in the Journal. Use a **star**, not a heart (the heart already means "Love this!").
- **Jump to:** a button in All photos that lists years and months, so nobody has to scroll through hundreds of photos.
- **Share several sunsets at once:** pick a few in All photos (say, five from one trip) and send them together. For the MVP, sharing stays on Entry Detail, one tap from the Journal, so people see what they're sending.
- **On this day:** "A year ago today…" memories of past sunsets.
- **Season recaps:** a look back at a season's sunsets (Spotify Wrapped-style).

## Capturing and storytelling
- **Several photos per sunset** (a carousel).
- **Golden-hour daily moment:** a nudge to capture tonight's sunset (BeReal-style).
- **Sunset reminders:** a notification shortly before local sunset (PRD 6.8; needs the app installed to the home screen on iPhone).
- **Rare Sightings badges:** "Sun rays", "Alpenglow", "Green flash" and others, with a collection in the Journal. The best candidate for something on the photo, instead of a number.

## Friends and sharing
- **Friends / all reactions in one place:** a bottom-bar tab showing every reaction and note across all your shared sunsets, newest first.
- **Voice notes from friends** as reactions.
- **Send one back:** a friend replies with their own sunset.
- **Reply to a friend's reaction.**
- **Notification when a friend reacts.**
- **Dead-link page:** a kind message when a share link was stopped or the sunset deleted.
- **One-tap Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok and Facebook buttons** (need a native phone app).
- **Home-screen widget** for a close circle (Locket-style).
- **In-app friend circles** and a **public story feed** (Phase 2).

## Live Map
- **Live Map and "Post to map"** (Phase 6). The bottom bar grows from Journal · You to Journal · Map · You (and a Friends tab when that arrives). New sunset stays at the top of the screen.
- **Community voting** that keeps great sunsets on the map longer.

## Account and settings
- **Settings:** AI story help on/off, delete all recordings, notification choices.
- **First-time welcome tour** and **"Add to Home Screen"** guidance.
- **Account deletion and data export.**

## Scoring
- **Weighted or trained scoring** once there's real data.
- **A community score** alongside the automatic one.

## Business
- **Printed photo books, calendars and prints** of a person's sunsets.
- **Premium tier** and **brand partnerships** (travel, wellness, mindfulness).
